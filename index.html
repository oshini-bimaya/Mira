ABOUT MIRA

A creative space for NSBM student artists.

MIRA is a creative platform for NSBM students to showcase their talents in art and handcraft. It provides a space for student creators to share their artwork, handmade creations, and creative ideas with the wider NSBM community. Students can discover talented creators, explore different forms of art, and participate in exhibitions and creative showcases.

MESSAGE FROM THE VICE CHANCELLOR

Celebrating creativity beyond the classroom.

“At NSBM, we believe that student talent extends far beyond academics. MIRA provides a platform for our students to express their creativity, showcase their talents in art and handcraft, and inspire the wider university community through their imagination and passion.

Through creativity, students have the opportunity to express their ideas, develop confidence, and connect with others who share similar interests.

MIRA celebrates the artistic talents of NSBM students and encourages them to continue exploring their creativity beyond the classroom.”

Vice Chancellor - Prof. E. A. Weerasinghe NSBM Green University
